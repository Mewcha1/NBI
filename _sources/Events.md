## January 23, 2026: Student Team Wins Poster Presentation

FGCU software engineering students Michael Alvear, Luis Cabrera, Mark Schwartz, and Christopher Smith presented *“Large Language Models for the Democratization of Groundwater and Climate Data: An Example of Groundwater Climate Resiliency in South Florida”* at the 35th Annual Southwest Florida Water Resources Conference. The students demonstrated an AI-enabled groundwater and climate dashboard that allows users to search, visualize, interpret, and download project data through natural-language queries. Their work received **third place in the student poster competition**, recognizing the team’s interdisciplinary contribution to making groundwater and climate information more accessible to researchers, decision-makers, and South Florida communities.![Poster_Win](Poster_Win.png)

### June 16–17, 2025: Seminole Tribe of Florida Engagement
The project team visited Immokalee, the Brighton Reservation, and Big Cypress to present preliminary findings from Task 1 and engage with Seminole Tribe representatives and staff. The meetings and field discussions provided opportunities to exchange perspectives on regional environmental and hydrological priorities, identify areas of mutual research interest, and strengthen collaboration between the project team and the Seminole Tribe of Florida.
